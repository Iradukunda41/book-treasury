About

About Book Treasury

Book Treasury is a small book discovery app. Browse real books, search by title and keep a list of your favorites, all in one place.

How it works

1

Search

Type a title and Book Treasury asks the Open Library API for matching books, with their covers.

2

Save

Tap the heart or "Add to Favorites". Your list is kept in your browser, so it is still there when you come back.

3

Come back

Open the Favorites page to see your saved books. The number next to Favorites in the menu always shows how many you saved.

What you can do

  • Search books by title
  • See each book's cover, author and year
  • Save books to your favorites and remove them any time
  • Use it on your phone, tablet or computer

Where the books come from

Every book, author and cover shown in Book Treasury comes from Open Library, an open online library catalog.
